leading punctuation. The corpus is ASCII transliteration with inline
glosses, so the gate passes lines that Fish will actually speak with
raw Pali in them. Re-ran the audit this session with a gloss-attachment
rule (Pali token must be followed within 70 chars by a dash or paren):

- S2b5  paticca samuppada (never glossed in line)
- S2b7  avijja (gloss paraphrases later in the sentence, not attached)
- S2b8  abhisaikhata (gloss attaches to the second word of the compound
        — borderline; likely fine when spoken, but the rule flags it)
- S2b13 yadidam (never glossed)
- S3b8  asava (glossed as ", influx" — check: pass if comma-gloss counts)
- S3b13 janami passami tath' eva etam (Pali phrase, never glossed)
- S3b15 yathabhuta (never glossed)
- S5b2  FULL PALI SENTENCE — "Dhono na hi tena mannati yadidam
        ditthasutam mutesu va — ..." The worst case: unquoted leading
        Pali, and the lead-drop only strips quoted Pali. This is the
        must-fix of the group.
- S5b11 satipatthana (gloss "four ways of paying attention" exists in
        the key but is not attached inline)

Fix shape (for the amendment pass): (1) generalize the lead-drop to
