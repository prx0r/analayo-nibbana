## Verdict in one paragraph
The spine holds: a first-time listener gets true answers to all six
questions — stilling (motto), blindness (dog), fuel (craving-gap),
construction (mirror), method (Bahiya), remainder (grown-cool) — and the
two best-covered sermons (15 Bahiya, 18 two-elements) prove the method
works when applied. But coverage is uneven by thirds: sermons 1–8 keep
their slogans while losing their arguments (island arrival, fire
catechism, unestablished-consciousness practice, cinema engine, Kevaddha
verse, cord/shuttle); sermons 9–16 keep their conclusions while losing
their precision instruments (eightfold etiology, contact hybrid,
Nibbana-as-designation, signless samadhi, elements/atammayata);
sermons 25–33 keep their vocabulary while losing their instructions
(element-body, signless development, peg substitution, 36 pathways,
balancing, twofold view, gap mechanics). Nothing here is misrepresented;
much that is load-bearing is simply absent. Ranked gaps + recommended
B-promotions below — all sourced, none applied (owner picks).
