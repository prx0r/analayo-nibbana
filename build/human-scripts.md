# HUMAN RECORDING SCRIPT (position: AFTER the answer, optional) — DRAFT for your voice/approval. ~4 min total. No Pali (Analayo owns it).

Read each cold, one breath between sentences. These open their section, then the bell, then Fish + Analayo take over.

## §1 — What it is (~35s)
What is Nibbana? Not a place, and not annihilation — an extinction, like a fire going out for lack of fuel. This section is the definition everything else hangs on. Listen for the fire, the thirst quenched, and what remains when grasping stops.

## §2 — How suffering gets made (~40s)
How does suffering get made? Not across lifetimes first — in this moment: consciousness and name-and-form leaning on each other like two bundles of reeds. Listen for the whirlpool, the ruler that doesn't fit inside time, and the middle way between "it exists" and "it doesn't".

## §3 — Why this feels real (~40s)
Why does this feel real? Because the mind names, reckons, and proliferates — honey-ball delight, wrong-turn roads, imagining earth as mine. Listen for how delight becomes grasping, and grasping becomes the next moment of me.

## §4 — What the self is made of (~40s)
What is the self made of? A self — out of mirror-images, signs, and the last hiding place: the watcher itself. Listen for the consciousness with nowhere to land, and what happens when you look for the observer and don't find one.

## §5 — How to see through the self (~45s)
How do you see through the self? Bare sensing — in the seen, only the seen — held lightly like a razor, practised as mindfulness, until even the raft gets left behind. This is the practice section. Listen with your hands, not just your ears.

## §6 — What remains (~35s)
What remains when it stops? Embers cooling to ash, an ocean too deep to measure, a lotus out of a cesspool, faculties brought to completion. Listen for an ending that isn't an ending — and then silence.
