And I think the trick is:

> **Each path is one question.  
> Each question has one dominant visual logic.  
> Each path keeps reappearing and deepening across the film.**

# Nibbāna — 6-path visual engine

## Path 1 — **What is Nibbāna?**
### Core law:
**Black screen + silence** is the base answer.

This is the cleanest one.

Then around that, you can have **answer-images of cessation**:
- candle going out
- bubble popping
- light switched off
- sound fading into silence
- ember losing glow
- ripple flattening
- eyes closing

## Why it works
This path is not trying to define Nibbāna conceptually.
It is giving the viewer **felt analogies of cessation**.

## Best role in the film
- opens the film
- returns between sections
- ends the film

## Important distinction
At the **beginning**, black = **question**.  
At the **end**, black = **answer / invitation / mirror**.

That's beautiful.

---

## Path 2 — **Why don't I see it now?**
### Core law:
The film itself **distracts the viewer in real time**.

This is brilliant.

You're not just saying "the world distracts us."
You are actually **demonstrating** it.

## Visual field
This path should be filled with:
- beautiful women / attraction
- scrolling feeds
- children laughing
- romance
- money / status / success
- food
- conflict / drama
- crowds
- novelty
- celebrations
- sports
- shopping
- screens
- fantasy
- memory
- fear
- beauty

Basically:
**all the compelling hooks of life**

## Why it works
Because the point is not:
"these things are evil."

The point is:
**attention is constantly pulled outward and captured.**

The viewer gets caught by the very images meant to explain capture.

That's very strong.

## Sound treatment
- seductive
- emotionally rich
- rhythmic
- fast-cutting at times
- layered
- maybe increasingly overstimulating

## Risk
Don't make it moralistic or anti-life.
It's not "women and children are the problem."
It's:
**fascination, stimulation, drama, and captivation obscure clear seeing.**

## Best daily-life triggers
- reaching for phone
- getting pulled into beauty / fantasy
- getting swept into emotional drama

---

## Path 3 — **What keeps it going?**
### Core law:
**The spinning top.**

This is your main mechanism.
Honestly, I think this becomes one of the great inventions of the whole film.

## Visual logic
The top appears throughout the film:
- already spinning
- beautiful, hypnotic
- recurring
- sometimes slowing
- then later somehow spinning again

Only near the end do we discover:
**a hand re-spins it.**

Then the viewer understands:
- the process doesn't just continue
- it is **fed**
- and it may be **we who feed it**

## Why it works
It gives you:
- suspense
- repetition
- reveal
- doctrine
- rewatch value
- a visual "engine" for samsaric continuation

## Supporting companion clips
To support the top, you can occasionally echo the same principle with:
- someone scratching an itch
- reloading feed
- relighting cigarette
- feeding fire
- rewinding toy
- nudging pendulum

But the **top stays the master image**.

---

## Path 4 — **How is all this being built?**
This is the one you forgot, and I think this is the right slot for it.

### Core law:
**Construction. Fabrication. Making.**

If Path 2 is distraction and Path 3 is continuation,  
Path 4 is:

> **How does "my world" get assembled in the first place?**

## Visual field
- mirror / self-image
- labels on objects
- face recognition box locking onto a face
- makeup / grooming / becoming someone
- assembling a profile
- building a room
- knot being tied
- puppet being strung
- border drawn around a shape
- map drawn over territory
- social identity being put on
- objects sorted into categories
- a mask being worn

## Why it works
This path answers:
- how "me"
- how "mine"
- how "world"
- how "story"
get produced.

This gives the film a section about **fabrication**, not just distraction.

## Sound treatment
More precise and "constructed":
- clicks
- labels
- little locking sounds
- accumulating layers
- perhaps calmer than Path 2, but more analytic

## Risk
This one can become too clever.
So keep the images **plain and legible**.

## Best interpretation
This is the path of:
**naming, dividing, identifying, claiming, constructing.**

---

## Path 5 — **How is Nibbāna realized?**
Or if you really want the blunt version:
**How do I attain Nibbāna?**

I'd slightly prefer:
**How is it realized?**
because "attain" can sound like getting a thing.

### Core law:
**Uprooting, discipline, simplification, right effort, and guidance.**

This is where your "teachers / effort / clean / discipline / enthusiasm" idea belongs.

## Visual field
- uprooting weeds from the root
- sweeping floor
- washing bowl
- unclenching fist
- cutting a vine at the root
- sharpening a blade
- tying robe / preparing seat
- waking before dawn
- walking meditation
- sitting upright
- repeated returning
- lighting a lamp carefully
- careful cleaning
- monk / teacher / mentor / instruction
- student listening attentively
- someone stopping the hand before it feeds the top again

## Why it works
This path answers:
- not by spectacle
- not by getting something exotic
- but through purification, steadiness, restraint, uprooting, practice

## Important nuance
This should not look like:
- hustle culture
- self-help optimization montage
- macho conquest

It should look like:
- clear effort
- steadiness
- discipline
- joy in simplicity
- careful training

## Sound treatment
- cleaner
- more spacious
- rhythmic, but not frenetic
- maybe percussive and precise
- teacher voice can appear here more than elsewhere

## Strongest master image
Honestly:
**uprooting a weed** is fantastic.
Very doctrinally right.

Also:
**the hand almost moving to re-spin the top, but stopping**
could be one of the greatest moments in the whole piece.

---

## Path 6 — **What remains?**
### Core law:
**After the feeding stops, what is left?**

This is the return, the quieting, the aftermath.

## Visual field
- still pond
- quiet room
- open sky
- empty stage
- extinguished candle with a little smoke
- a loose rope after tension is gone
- a cleaned space
- sunlight in an ordinary room
- an unspun top resting
- black screen
- silence

## Why it works
This path should not answer too aggressively.
It should not become:
"here is the big mystical finale."

It should feel like:
- less pressure
- less compulsion
- less noise
- less becoming

## Best relation to Path 1
Path 1 and Path 6 should mirror each other.

### Path 1:
black = question

### Path 6:
black = lived answer / unresolved mirror / stillness

That's the formal poetry of the whole film.

# So the six paths are:

## 1. **What is Nibbāna?**
Black, silence, cessation images.

## 2. **Why don't I see it now?**
Distraction, fascination, seduction, life-drama.

## 3. **What keeps it going?**
The spinning top; the hidden hand that re-spins it.

## 4. **How is all this being built?**
Fabrication of self/world through naming, division, identity, construction.

## 5. **How is Nibbāna realized?**
Uprooting, discipline, right effort, simplification, teachers, training.

## 6. **What remains?**
Quiet, release, no re-feeding, ordinary stillness, black silence.
