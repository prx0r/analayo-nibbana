# THE INTENT — what this project is, in one place.
Collated from the full build conversation + owner messages + process
notes, 2026-10-25. This is the north star; everything else in `process/`

---

## 1. The work

Take a long teaching series (audio + its text) and produce the clearest
possible path through it — not a shorter playlist, but an *argument*:
six built sections that explain the thing so a first-time listener
gets it, at four depths. The first build is Ñāṇananda's Nibbāna
sermons via Analayo's lectures; the template ports to any audio+text
(Bettina Bäumer's talks, grace, …).

Owner's words: *"the most concise explainer and path through these
logically"* · *"the ultimate plain english compression"* · *"a path
through like our 6 that basically elucidate the text clearly"*

## 2. The gospel (never reordered)

Every unit of content is exactly three layers, in this order:

    TEXT VERBATIM → PLAIN ENGLISH → AUDIO (archive voice)

Owner: *"we always want nanananda verbatim. then underneath plain
english then underneath analayo … first comes TEXT VERBATIM, then
PLAIN ENGLISH TRANSLATION then AUDIO speaker"*

Nothing else exists. No summaries, no bridges, no composed prose —
not in files, not in chat (axiom 16 + my R1 reflex). The archive voice
(Analayo / Bäumer) reads AND explains; Fish speaks key-mappings only;
the owner's hand only writes intros and questions.

## 3. The spine = questions

Sections are questions, not topics. A spine question passes the FORMULA
(process/12-ARGUMENT):

1. **Standalone + precise** — cold read, subject *named* and named as
   the section's material answers it. No naked pronouns.
   (owner: *"is any of it real??? is any of what real?"*)
2. **The cut** — turns the previous answer into tension for THIS
   listener, now. (owner: *"cutting insightful question"* e.g.
   *"why do i not see nibbana right now"*)
3. **Content-fit** — that section's essay 0 answers it aloud, no leap.
4. **Plain** — everyday words, ≤9, Fish says it flat. A kick-off, not
   an essay: *"there should only be a few leading in questions that
   make sense to kick off the enquiry"*

Chained, they form ONE enquiry (each answer is the next question's
premise; pronouns resolve in-chain). Current chain:
What is Nibbana? → Why don't I see Nibbana right now? → Why does the
world feel real? → Am I real too? → How do I stop adding myself? →
What's left when the world stops?

Questions are the ONE thing humans author (owner picks; tests inform,
never decide — *"they aren't hard gates come on be smart"*). Answers are
never authored: verbatim only.

## 4. Essay 0 — the gold dust

Per section: the compressed answer that still explains fully. Built
FIRST (owner: *"we should build essay 0 first next time and build out
from there"*), as a **micro-session**: question and answer written and
read *together* — *"write it all together as a micro session question
answer so it makes sense"*.

- The oral test: read Q, then A, out loud. Any leap = rewrite OUR
  question (never the verbatim).
- It **seeds** essays a → b → c: the expansion opens facets of the
  seed, never swaps in new ideas (the UNFOLDING law, from the
  Bäumer repo: *the essay performs its doctrine*).
- Recurs 4× (seed, core, gold, pool) with a layer-bell at each
  re-entry — repetition IS the learning.
- ~20 seconds, provenance-checked, a whole section's fate hangs on it.
  (owner: *"essay 0 should basically be gold dust"*)

Owner-validated variants so far: **V3 approved** (*"ur v3 was good"*) —
*"What does Nibbana end?"* → *"the destruction of this very mass of
suffering which is just before us."* V1 (the motto) is the built
default. V2 died as a category error (*"Where does Nibbana go?? that
makes no sense"* — Nibbana IS the going-out; only fire/muni go).

## 5. The ritual

Every section: **motto-bell → Fish asks the question → 3s recollect
(the listener answers first) → essay 0 on screen → archive voice
answers.** Nothing wedges between question and answer — no intro
speaks in that slot (owner: *"intor = what is nibbana fish correct.
then analayo reading out this is peaceful"*).

Two bells only: motto-bell (bright, with "this is excellent" — the
motif: *"this is peaceful is the motif"* … *"bell one is simply that
phrase stilling right thats the most compressed form of what it is"*)
and zoom-bell (6s, layer change). Repetition happens at three scales:
the seed chain, each section's re-open, each layer's recurrence.

## 6. The depths

essay 0 (the seed, ~20s) → essay **a** (core, ~93m total) → essay
**b** (gold, 6.3h) → essay **c** (pool, 20.7h) — same argument at
widening radius. Listened as: **seed first** (six micro-sessions
chained, ~4 min — this is where chain pronouns live), then zoom into
each section which re-anchors its own Q+0 and grows outward.

## 7. Roles

- **Owner** — picks (question sets, essay-0 candidates, mixes, ears).
  Tests give evidence; taste is theirs. Their rejections are the data.
- **Archive voice** (Analayo/Bäumer) — reads + explains, owns all
  scripture-aloud. Untouched.
- **Fish** — key-mappings only (≤5 words, glosses); never speaks Pali,
  never composes.
- **Me (the machine)** — pipeline, gates, swaps, notes. My one hard
  limit: never generate content-voice. My failure modes and their
  counter-rules: `process/RECURRING-MISTAKES.md` (R1 prose-reflex first).

## 8. What gates are for (and what they're not)

Gates guard the FLOOR: provenance (every sacred word exists somewhere,
exact), stale paths, slop canaries, my memory-claims. They never encode
taste — a passing gate is permission to have an opinion, not a verdict.
Owner: *"they arent hard gates"* — the question-gate evidence informs
the pick; the four tests live in judgment, run by humans.
