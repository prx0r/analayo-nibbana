# SERMON 20 — EXCAVATION

Source: Nibbana-20.pdf (BCBS/Dharma Seed, free). 8256 words.

---

## SECTION 1: WHAT NIBBANA IS

### The unshakeable deliverance as supreme
"This is the highest knowledge, this is the unsurpassed bliss, this
sorrow-less, taintless security, is the supreme debtless-ness."
Analayo parallel: MĀ 125.

### Debtless-ness (kamma as debt)
"Samsara or reiterated existence is itself a debt, which one can never
pay off. When one comes to think of kamma and its result, it is a debt
that keeps on gathering an interminable interest."
"Even from this debt the arahants have won freedom by destroying the
seeds of kamma, by rendering them infertile."

### Ratanasutta: whatever is old is extinct
"Whatever is old is extinct and there is no arising anew. The karmic
debt is paid off and there is no fresh incurring."

## SECTION 2: HOW THE MECHANISM WORKS

### The Kutūhalasālā (Vacchagotta's report)
"There was a meeting of recluses, brahmins and wandering ascetics in
the debating hall. They all declare categorically the after death state
of both types of their disciples. But as for this ascetic Gotama, in
the case of a disciple who has attained the highest state of perfection,
he does not predicate his destiny according to the tetralemma."
Analayo parallel: SĀ 957, SĀ2 190 (no six teachers mentioned).

### The Buddha's formula (the correct answer)
"He cut off craving, disjoined the fetter, and by rightly understanding
conceit for what it is, made an end of suffering."
"When craving is cut off, the guide-in-becoming, which is responsible
for rebirth, is done away with."

### The flame flung by wind (Kutūhalasālā)
"When a flame flung by the wind goes even far, that has wind as its
object of grasping. At that time wind itself serves as the object of
grasping."
"When a being lays down this body and has reached a certain body, I
say, he has craving as his grasping."
Analayo parallel: SĀ 957 (Chinese).

### The reading anuppatto vs anuppanno
"We have adopted the reading anuppatto, 'has reached', as more plausible.
The commentary seeks to justify the reading anuppanno, 'is not reborn'."
"Anuppatto avoids those syntactical difficulties. Our interpretation
rules out even the possibility of an antarabhava."

### The Aggivacchagotta fire catechism (full)
"Suppose a fire were burning before you, would you know 'this fire is
burning before me'?"
"What does this fire burn in dependence on?" — "Grass and sticks."
"When that fire was extinguished, to which direction did it go, east,
west, north or south?"
"That wouldn't do as a reply. The fire burned in dependence on its fuel.
Being without fuel, it is reckoned as extinguished."

### The palm stump (the Tathagata's aggregates)
"That form by which one designating the Tathagata might designate him,
that has been abandoned by him, cut off at the root, made like an
uprooted palm tree, made non-existent and incapable of arising again."
"The Tathagata is free from reckoning in terms of form, deep, immeasurable
and hard to fathom, like the great ocean."

### The ten undetermined points
The Buddha laid aside, rejected, and refused to answer categorically:
1-2. World eternal / not eternal
3-4. World finite / infinite
5-6. Soul = body / soul ≠ body
7-10. Tathagata exists / doesn't / both / neither after death

### Why the questions are ill-founded
"Each of them is just a speculative view, a jungle of views, a desert
of views, a distortion of views, an aberration of views, a fetter of
views, fraught with suffering... It does not lead to disenchantment,
to dispassion, to cessation, to Nibbana."
"They occur as real problems only to the untaught worldling."

### Loka = disintegrating (the world redefined)
"It is disintegrating, monk, that is why it is called 'the world'."
"Whatever is subject to disintegration is called 'the world' in the
noble one's discipline."

## SECTION 3: WHAT KEEPS THE MECHANISM RUNNING

### Craving as bhavanetti (guide in becoming)
"Like a promontory, it juts out into the ocean of samsara. When it
comes to rebirth, it is craving that bridges the apparent gap."

### The tetralemma as lawyer's trap
"This is an attempt to determine the world in temporal terms... The
next set of alternatives seeks to determine the world in spatial terms."
"The clever lawyers put to a respondent a question like 'have you now
given up drinking?' Either way tends to create a wrong impression."

---

## SECTION 4: WHAT THE MECHANISM BUILDS

### The five aggregates abandoned by craving
"Rādha, you give up that desire, that lust, that delight, that craving
for form. It is thus that form comes to be abandoned, cut off at the
root, made like an uprooted palm tree."

## SECTION 5: HOW TO SEE THROUGH IT

### Vacchagotta's confusion as the entry
"At this unexpected response of the Buddha, Vacchagotta confesses that
he is fully confused and bewildered. The Buddha grants that his confusion
is understandable, since this Dhamma is so deep and subtle that it cannot
be plumbed by logic."

### The fire catechism as practice
The Buddha walks Vacchagotta through: fire burns on fuel → goes out
when fuel is exhausted → "where did it go?" is the wrong question →
craving is the fuel for rebirth.

## REFUTATIONS

### The commentary on antarabhava
"Some scholars have exploited this commentarial explanation to postulate
a theory of antarabhava, or interim existence, prior to rebirth proper.
Our interpretation, based on the reading anuppatto, rules out even the
possibility of an antarabhava."

### The commentary on accutam (Dhammapada)
"The commentator brings in the word sassatam, 'eternal', for accutam,
giving the idea that the arahants go to an eternal place of rest."

## PASS 2 FINDS

- The fire catechism in full (Aggivacchagotta) is the most precise
  teaching on why the tetralemma is wrong — the fire question walks
  Vacchagotta through the mechanism step by step.
- The palm stump simile is the most striking image of the arahant's
  aggregates — abandoned, cut off at the root, uprooted but still
  standing.
- The ocean simile (deep, immeasurable, hard to fathom) is the final
  statement on why the Tathagata can't be categorized.
- The loka definition (disintegrating) redefines the world itself as
  process, not substance.
- The Kutūhalasālā report shows that the Buddha's refusal to answer
  was famous and puzzling even in his own time.

## PASS 3: HOLES AND CONNECTIONS

- **Hole:** The relationship between the ten undetermined points and
  the practice path is established but not worked through. The practice
  is the fire catechism itself — see the fire go out, see craving as
  fuel.
- **Connection to Sermon 19:** The oil lamp simile is the fire
  catechism. The assaddho pun verse is the same insight — faith
  becomes redundant when knowledge arrives.
- **Connection to Sermon 18:** The lotus practice is the arahant who
  "cannot be reckoned" — free from designation.
- **Connection to VBT pack:** The fire catechism IS the VBT fire
  contemplation practice. The palm stump IS the concept-deconstruction
  practice — cut off at the root.
